Section 1 — Roster breakdown

1.1 Positional breakdown

16 total players.

Starters: QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, FLEX, TE, D/ST, K

Reserves: 7 bench spots

1.2 Keepers

Before each season, each owner may retain one player from their previous season's roster — one or none, never more. The keeper is "drafted" in the final round of the draft.

Rookies are ineligible to be added prior to the draft and cannot be used as keepers. Violation is subject to draft pick forfeit.

Keepers are announced to the league publicly. The commissioner sets the retaining deadline and the submission window; once submitted, keepers cannot be changed.

If a keeper retires after being announced, the owner regains the draft value that player cost but may not select a replacement keeper.

1.3 Trading

Owners may exchange players and draft picks during the trade window set by the commissioner. Trades proceeding in good faith, with no foul play, are approved. All trades are reviewed and processed within 24 hours.

1.3.1 Trade collusion

All trades are reviewed to rule out bribery or collusion between owners. Confirmed violations are punished as follows:

  1. First offense — loss of the ability to keep players for one season.
  2. Second offense — removal from the league.

Trades found to involve collusion are reversed or denied. If the trade affected the outcome of prior games, those results are revised. A first offense clears from an owner's record after three seasons.

The commissioner may reverse or deny any trade at any time to protect league integrity, and may consult third parties for additional review.

1.3.2 Trade deadline

The trading market opens immediately after the draft. The trade deadline itself is set by the commissioner each season.

1.3.3 Veto / trade process

A trade is vetoed only if at least 75% of the league (9 of 12 owners) votes in favor of the veto.

1.4 Waiver wire

Waiver order starts the season in reverse order of the draft, then resets weekly to reverse standings order.

Free agency is locked prior to the draft. Any player added before the draft is removed from the roster immediately.

1.5 Eliminated teams

Transactions are locked for eliminated teams. A team is "eliminated" once it is ineligible for 5th place — i.e. it has fallen into the consolation bracket.

Section 2 — Ownership expectations

2.1 League fee

Each team pays a $20 entry fee before the draft. Owners who miss the payment deadline risk losing ownership of their team. Multi-owner teams may split the fee.

2.2 Selling your team

Owners wishing to leave the league must notify the commissioner before the draft date is set.

2.3 Removal

Owners may vote to remove any owner at any time if that owner is judged to be harming league integrity, backed by acceptable evidence (subject to third-party interpretation). The commissioner replaces a removed owner.

A removed owner receives a league fee refund if removal happens before the season starts (the season begins once the draft is complete); no refund if removal happens mid-season. If an owner is removed mid-season, the commissioner manages that roster until a replacement owner is found, and no transactions are allowed on that team in the meantime.

2.4 Voting

New rules are proposed and voted on at the league draft each season. Proposals must be raised before the draft starts and require a majority vote (more than 50%, i.e. 6 of 12) to pass.

2.5 Prize money

1st place: $180 · 2nd place: $40 · 3rd place: $20

2.6 Last place punishment

The last-place finisher must play a full round of golf without a ball.

Section 3 — Drafting

3.1 Draft order

Draft order runs in reverse order of the previous season's final standings.

3.2 Draft day trades

Trades made during the draft should be processed through ESPN. If a trade can't be processed live, the commissioner resolves it after the fact.

3.3 Draft date, location & time

The commissioner proposes multiple dates and times; the option with the most owner availability becomes the official draft date. Owners who can't attend must arrange and notify the commissioner of a proxy drafter, or will be auto-drafted.

Section 4 — Scoring system

12-team, full points-per-reception (PPR) league. The full scoring breakdown lives on the ESPN Fantasy app.

4.1 Voting on scoring system

Owners can propose scoring changes at the draft each season. A well-received proposal goes to a league vote and passes with a majority (50%).

Section 5 — Postseason

5.1 Playoffs

Six teams make the playoffs. The top two seeds by record receive a first-round bye. Every playoff round is a single-week matchup; the winner advances.

5.2 Playoff tiebreakers

Playoff ties are broken by bench points scored.

5.3 Seeding tiebreakers

Regular-season ties are broken in this order: (1) head-to-head record, (2) points for, (3) total points against.

Section 6 — Amendments

6.1 Future changes

Changes are proposed, discussed, and voted on at the start of the draft. Unless otherwise noted, proposed changes need a 7/12 majority to pass and take effect the following season.
